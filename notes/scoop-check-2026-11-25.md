Closest prior work and how this differs:
- SECCO (Bellazzini et al. 2015, https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5857): LBT ground imaging of 25 of the 59 clouds to r~26.5; no counterpart within ~1.5 Mpc. This test uses Euclid space imaging (point-source selection at faint magnitudes) with an NIR colour filter, for every cloud inside DR1.
- Sand et al. 2015 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.00720): archival search of SDSS/GALEX-depth images.
- Gatto et al. 2026 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16025): blind Euclid DR1 search for resolved systems; not targeted at HI clouds and does not give limits at UCHVC positions.
